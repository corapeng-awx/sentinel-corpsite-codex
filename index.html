How it works

Multi-stage AI decisioning in milliseconds

Every transaction flows through a real-time pipeline that scores, decides, and optimises without slowing checkout.

01

Profile and model selection

Each transaction is mapped to specialised risk profiles based on business type and payment context. Digital goods, travel, subscriptions, and high-risk categories each get tuned models.

02

Feature computation and risk scoring

Thousands of features computed per transaction across six dimensions: device and biometrics, behavioural patterns, network and identity, graph analysis, issuer intelligence, and payment context.

Device fingerprintBehavioural biometricsVelocity analysisGraph clusteringIP geolocationImpossible travelEmulator detectionIssuer preferences

03

Layered rule evaluation

Risk scores flow through layered rules: fraud pattern rules that adapt to emerging threats, segment-specific rules per vertical, scheme compliance rules, and your own merchant-specific strategies.

04

End-to-end payment optimisation

Fraud decisions feed into Optimize 360 to orchestrate the complete payment journey. Smart 3DS authentication, intelligent acquiring path routing, and adaptive retry logic, all informed by fraud signals.
